Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for these short courses?

Anyone with a basic educational background can apply. The IT Education course is designed for school children, while other courses are open to students, professionals, and general learners.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. All students who successfully complete their course will receive an official certificate from the University of Turbat.

What is the mode of instruction?

All courses are conducted on-campus with hands-on lab sessions. Schedules are designed to accommodate both full-time and part-time learners.

How do I pay the course fee?

After submitting the online form, download your admission PDF and visit the university office for physical payment verification.
