Short Course Program Overview

The University of Turbat offers practical short courses designed to build specialized skills, professional certification readiness, and digital literacy for a wide range of learners. Each course is tailored with focused learning outcomes, flexible duration, and hands-on practice.

IT Education for School Children

Duration: 2 Months

This course introduces children to computing, digital safety, and introductory programming in a fun and age-appropriate format. It builds confidence with technology while teaching students how to use educational tools safely.

  • Basic computer skills, internet safety, and visual programming concepts.
  • Interactive learning designed for primary and middle school learners.
  • Encourages creativity through digital storytelling, simple coding, and multimedia.

AI Automation

Duration: 2 Months

This course is a practical introduction to AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and other modern assistants. Students learn prompt engineering, building AI-enabled mobile apps, web apps, software, and productivity workflows for business, education, and office environments.

  • Key topics: prompt design, AI tool workflows, product ideation, and productivity automation.
  • Hands-on practice using AI to build mobile apps, web apps, software tools, and business solutions.
  • Learn how to create sellable products and services using AI tools for students, teachers, offices, and entrepreneurs.

MS Office Certification

Duration: 2 Months

Designed to prepare students for Microsoft Office certification, this course develops advanced skills in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook with an emphasis on productivity and workplace readiness.

  • Advanced functions, document design, data analysis, and presentation development.
  • Certification preparation exercises and exam-style practice tasks.
  • Recommended for students, office staff, teachers, and entry-level professionals.

DiT (Diploma in IT)

Duration: 12 Months

The Diploma in IT is an intensive foundational program covering computer fundamentals, networking, programming concepts, and basic database management.

  • Topics include operating systems, hardware, internet technologies, and introductory coding.
  • Focus on practical skills for everyday IT tasks and entry-level technical roles.
  • Good for learners seeking a strong base in information technology.

Professional Typing

Duration: 1 Month

A practical typing course that improves speed, accuracy, and familiarity with modern keyboard layouts. Ideal for administrative staff, students, and anyone looking to improve document entry efficiency.

  • Touch typing drills, speed-building exercises, and accuracy improvement techniques.
  • Focus on both English and Urdu typing practice where available.
  • Completion leads to a measurable improvement in everyday typing tasks.
