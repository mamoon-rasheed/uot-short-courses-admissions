Volunteer Developer Program

Bridge the gap between academic theory and professional reality. Join the UoT IT Team to build, manage, and lead.

The Volunteer Developer Program (VDP) is designed for students who want to go beyond the classroom. As an internee, you will work directly alongside the official IT Team, contributing to live University projects, maintaining infrastructure, and creating valuable resources for your peers.

Available Tracks

💻

Full Stack Development

Work on maintaining and upgrading the University's core portals and management systems. We require dedication to coding standards and clean architecture.

Required Stack:
Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Backend: C# and ASP.NET (Only)
📝

Documentation & Education

Developers need good guides. Your role is to understand our existing software and create technical documentation, user manuals, and video tutorials for future students and staff.

Skills: Technical Writing, Video Recording, Clear Communication
🌐

Networking & Administration

Get your hands on the hardware. Assist in managing the campus network, troubleshooting connectivity issues, reporting bugs, and monitoring server analytics.

Skills: Networking Basics, Hardware Troubleshooting, Server Mgmt
🎨

Content Creation

The digital face of the University. Help us craft engaging content for the official website, ensuring information is up-to-date, accessible, and visually appealing.

Skills: Copywriting, Basic CMS Knowledge, Creativity
🌱

The Learner Track

Zero coding knowledge? No problem. This track is for students with pure passion who want to start their IT journey. You will be mentored to eventually join the development teams.

Requirement: Curiosity & Passion to Learn

Why Join Us?

  • Real World Experience: Stop working on dummy projects. Work on software used by thousands.
  • Mentorship: Get guidance from senior IT professionals.
  • Certification: Receive an internship certificate upon successful tenure.
  • Networking: Connect with the best technical minds on campus.
  • Skill Refinement: Master C#, ASP.NET, and enterprise networking.
  • Leadership: Learn to manage projects and lead teams.
